## While I'm already in the mindset of season change

But hey, while we're at it; why not take the opportunity to do some long overdue migrations and upgrades in other areas as well? Lo and behold, I present to you Volume 1 of my New Season Upgrades series: Going from Gmail to [Protonmail](https://pr.tn/ref/CJMW98F9)!

It all started when I accidentally opened the network tab on my idle Gmail index and saw to my horrors that the number of requests were well into the thousands(!), sending an abominable amount (and type, actually) of data _while doing nothing_! Not a reason for migrating in and by itself, but it served as a fine reminder on how much crap we put up with from various big tech companies just because migrating to something else is a bit of a hassle. Btw, I'm not here to tell you all the reasons why you _should_ ditch Gmail, if you're reading this blog I almost assume at least some part of you already want to, but the hassle of it all seems overwhelming. My deepest sympathy to you, and further: I even tried to transition to [Protonmail](https://pr.tn/ref/CJMW98F9) a few years ago and went back because I never managed to finish! The unknown unknowns out-bullied my capacity for friction, I guess. There was always one or two accounts or contacts I never quite reached with my filter-jitsu and labeling-fu.

But these days I find it's quite ~~easy~~doable (again, with [Protonmail](https://pr.tn/ref/CJMW98F9) – there's even an official [migration guide](https://proton.me/support/switch-from-gmail-to-proton) covering the whole ordeal). Here are some encouraging highlights

- You can connect your Gmail account, making your Gmail stuff available within your new inbox
- You can even (if you want/need) _send_ messages from your old Gmail
- Filtering is better than it used to be, and the included "Newsletter view" (available if you turn on Beta features as of writing) is quite promising

What it _doesn't_ give you is auto-filtering so sophisticated that you forget that your account gets filled up with junk you _never_ read anyway (Gmail default content tab bars, I'm looking at you). And that involves being a bit more intentional and actually unsubscribing and/or making filters to REMOVE stuff from your inbox (instead of just labeling and letting rot); but – I keep telling myself – I think it's worth it.

Good to be back all y'all!
